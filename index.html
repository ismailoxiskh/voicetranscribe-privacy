Privacy Policy for VoiceTranscribe & Translate

Last Updated and Effective Date: November 1, 2026
Summary: VoiceTranscribe ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. The application performs real-time speech transcription and translation. Your audio and transcripts are processed strictly to provide the requested transcription and translation functionality, and your history is stored locally on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VoiceTranscribe & Translate (Application ID: com.ismail.voicetranscribe) collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By installing and using VoiceTranscribe, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Permissions and Audio Processing

VoiceTranscribe requires specific device permissions to deliver its core features:

  • RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone Access): The microphone permission is required exclusively when you activate the microphone button to convert your spoken words into written text or translate speech in real time. We do not listen to, record, or monitor background conversations when the microphone is inactive.
  • On-Device and Ephemeral Voice Processing: Real-time speech recognition is processed through Android's system speech recognition service (Google Speech Services). When cloud transcription fallback is utilized, temporary audio snippets are uploaded strictly to generate textual transcripts and are not retained for long-term storage or surveillance.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Used solely to calculate live audio volume levels for the dynamic visual waveform and configure audio playback through device speakers.

3. Text Translation & External Communication

To provide simultaneous translation across 22 global languages, text generated from your speech or typed into the translation box is transmitted over secure, encrypted HTTPS connections to external translation APIs:

  • Google Translate Public API: Processes text strings to return instant translation in the selected target language.
  • MyMemory Translated API: Serves as a backup translation gateway if primary translation channels encounter network timeouts.

These translation queries contain only the text you choose to speak or translate and do not contain personal identifiers such as your phone number, device IMEI, or physical location.

4. User Profile and Registration Data

VoiceTranscribe does not require account creation, passwords, phone numbers, or social media logins to operate.

  • Display Name: Upon initial launch, the user is invited to provide a display name (e.g., "John" or "User") to personalize the application experience.
  • Local Storage: This name is saved locally on your device within encrypted sandbox storage (AsyncStorage).
  • Anonymous Installation Counter: When a user enters their name and starts the app, a one-time HTTPS request is transmitted to a private Google Apps Script endpoint to increment an aggregate user installation count in a Google Spreadsheet. No financial data, contacts, or passwords are transmitted.

5. Local Data Storage & User Control

Your privacy and control over your data are paramount:

  • Saved Transcripts & History: Any transcript or translation that you choose to save is stored locally in the application's isolated private sandbox directory on your device.
  • Data Deletion: You retain complete control over your saved records. You can delete individual transcript records at any time or use the "Clear All History" button in the History tab to permanently delete all saved entries immediately.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application completely erases all local database records, cache files, and user preferences from your device.

6. Third-Party Services and Analytics

VoiceTranscribe interfaces with the following third-party service providers solely to perform necessary app functions:

  • Google Speech Services (Android System): For real-time speech recognition.
  • Google Translate & MyMemory: For language translation services.
  • GitHub Gist: Used to periodically query a lightweight JSON configuration file to notify users if an updated version is available on Google Play or if scheduled server maintenance is ongoing.
  • Google Connectivity Check (generate_204): Used to verify active Internet connectivity before attempting cloud translations.

7. Advertising & Monetization

VoiceTranscribe currently does not contain any advertisements (no Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or third-party ad networks). We do not collect Google Advertising IDs (AAID), track user activity across third-party websites, or sell user information to data brokers.

8. Security and Encryption in Transit

All network communications between VoiceTranscribe and remote servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). This safeguards your data against unauthorized interception and eavesdropping during transit.

9. Children's Privacy

VoiceTranscribe is a general-purpose utility designed for adults, students, and professionals aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete such data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in application features or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted with an updated effective date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
